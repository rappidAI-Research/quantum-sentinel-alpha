## Uncertainties

- 177 groups have only weak textual correspondence between advisory and changed code; they are kept (the fix commit is referenced by a GitHub-reviewed advisory) but are the first candidates for review.
- 334 groups show no remediation pattern typical for their CWE family; this is a heuristic and is not an exclusion reason on its own.

- Localization is diff-derived: the changed lines of the fix or the insertion point of an added check.
