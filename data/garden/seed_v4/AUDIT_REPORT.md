## Uncertainties

- 280 groups have only weak textual correspondence between advisory and changed code; they are kept (the fix commit is referenced by a GitHub-reviewed advisory) but are the first candidates for review.
- 530 groups show no remediation pattern typical for their CWE family; this is a heuristic and is not an exclusion reason on its own.
- A post-fix record is a hard negative for the advisory's weakness only. Later fixes are found only when OSV links them to the same package with a commit reference.
- Localization is diff-derived: the changed lines of the fix or the insertion point of an added check.
